# Procureline launch video — production breakdown

**Format:** 1920×1080, 30fps, 46 seconds, landscape B2B launch film.  
**Audience:** university procurement leaders and procurement-heavy businesses.  
**Brand direction:** premium institutional dark mode: ink `#101a2a`, cloud `#edf5ef`, Procureline greens `#10a84f` and `#42e67a`, with calm mint `#b9f5c7`. The supplied product workspace and Procureline mark are the visual proof. Typography is `Arial` / `Arial Black` for reliable rendering.

## Rhythm
`pressure-build — BREAKTHROUGH — demonstrate — proof — resolve`

## Global rules

- A deep ink field, dotted planning-grid, soft green glows and drifting audit lines give each scene depth.
- Green is reserved for solved states, progress and the Procureline reveal; the problem uses restrained warm red.
- Motion is intentional: spreadsheet fragments pile up, then resolve into orderly blocks and reporting lines.
- Transitions use upward velocity and blur; the Procureline reveal is the single visual peak.

## Scene 1 — annual-plan pressure (0–7s)

**Concept:** A fiscal-year deadline starts with a desk full of unstable spreadsheets. The viewer feels the burden before seeing a solution.  
**Depth:** slow dotted grid and deadline ring; cascading workbook cards; foreground red exception markers.  
**Choreography:** cards DROP and JITTER; error stamps PULSE; headline types on.  
**Transition:** cards accelerate upward and blur into scene 2.

## Scene 2 — consolidation chaos (7–15s)

**Concept:** Department files, emails and broken formulae become a single tangled operation.  
**Depth:** ghost department labels, a red connecting line web, fragments of cells.  
**Choreography:** files CASCADE; the red web DRAWS; labels SHAKE; the core claim lands and holds.  
**Transition:** web collapses to one horizontal line.

## Scene 3 — Procureline enters (15–22s)

**Concept:** Order arrives: the Procureline mark and the platform transform the mess into a legible annual procurement plan.  
**Depth:** bright green radial bloom; actual supplied product screenshot inside an elevated canvas; logo as foreground hero.  
**Choreography:** bloom EXPANDS; screenshot ZOOMS through; logo rises; headline resolves.  
**Transition:** screenshot pushes toward the viewer into the product demonstration.

## Scene 4 — visible procurement (22–32s)

**Concept:** Hierarchy becomes something procurement teams can see and control: department, category, item.  
**Depth:** actual product screenshot; animated labelled block sequence; compliance ticker.  
**Choreography:** blocks SNAP together in sequence; product screen floats; compliance indicators FILL.  
**Transition:** blocks merge into an export sheet.

## Scene 5 — control at scale (32–40s)

**Concept:** The plan moves from fragmentation to an institution-wide source of truth.  
**Depth:** four benefit tiles, report grid and subtle audit lines.  
**Choreography:** benefits CASCADE; numbers count up visually; the result claim lands as a steady hold.  
**Transition:** all tiles fold into the final horizontal line.

## Scene 6 — call to action (40–46s)

**Concept:** A quiet, confident invitation to modernize procurement planning.  
**Depth:** oversized ghost wordmark, breathing green glow, logo lockup and one crisp rule.  
**Choreography:** mark FLOATS, CTA fades in, closing frame holds for two seconds.

## Recurring motifs

Planning-grid dots, green block corners, fine audit rules, and the transformation from scattered sheets to nested structure.

## Avoid

No generic stock people, no neon/cyberpunk language, no fake financial claims, no dense dashboard mockups competing with the supplied product screen.
